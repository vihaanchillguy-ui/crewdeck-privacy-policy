CrewDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

CrewDeck ("CrewDeck", "we", "us", or "our") is a workplace management application that helps businesses manage employees, rosters, time tracking, inventory, tasks, communications, and other workplace activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you use CrewDeck.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how CrewDeck is used, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account and login information
  • Profile picture, if provided
  • Business or workplace information
  • User role and position
  • Employee and team information
  • Roster and scheduled shift information
  • Availability and leave information
  • Clock-in and clock-out records
  • Break records
  • Hours worked and time corrections
  • Tasks and task completion information
  • Inventory, supplier and stocktake information
  • Workplace messages and announcements
  • Notifications and application activity
  • Other information users choose to enter into CrewDeck

2. How We Collect Information

We may collect information directly from users when they create accounts, join businesses, update profiles, use CrewDeck features, communicate through the application, or when authorised business administrators enter workplace information.

We may also receive information from third-party authentication or technical service providers when those services are used.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts
  • Connect employees with authorised businesses
  • Manage rosters and schedules
  • Record working hours and attendance
  • Manage employees, roles and teams
  • Manage inventory, suppliers and stocktakes
  • Manage workplace tasks
  • Provide workplace messaging and announcements
  • Send notifications
  • Provide dashboards and reports
  • Maintain and improve CrewDeck
  • Detect and prevent misuse, fraud and security issues
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements
  • Process subscriptions and payments where applicable

4. Workplace Data

CrewDeck is designed for workplace use. When you join a business, authorised administrators may be able to view and manage information associated with your workplace account depending on their role and permissions.

This may include your profile, role, roster, working hours, clock-in and clock-out records, leave or availability information, tasks, workplace messages and other workplace information.

5. PINs and Account Security

CrewDeck may use personal, temporary or workplace PINs for account security and access.

PIN information is intended to be protected using appropriate security measures. Users should not share their PINs with others.

Depending on their permissions, authorised business administrators may have access to PIN-management functions such as setting, resetting, generating temporary PINs, disabling or revoking employee PINs.

6. Messages and Communications

CrewDeck may provide direct messages, private group chats and workplace announcements.

Messages may be stored so authorised participants can access conversation history. Workplace administrators may have certain management capabilities depending on their role and permissions.

We do not sell private messages to advertisers.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to operate CrewDeck, including with service providers responsible for hosting, authentication, databases, payments, notifications, analytics, support and other technical services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or CrewDeck, or as part of a business transaction where legally permitted.

8. Third-Party Services

CrewDeck may use third-party services for authentication, cloud hosting, databases, payments, email, notifications, analytics, application infrastructure and other technical functions.

These providers may process information on our behalf and may have their own privacy policies.

9. Google Sign-In

If Google authentication is available and you choose to use it, CrewDeck may receive information such as your name, email address and profile information permitted by the authentication configuration.

CrewDeck does not receive your Google password.

10. Payments and Subscriptions

CrewDeck may offer paid subscriptions or other paid services.

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers or app-store payment systems. CrewDeck may receive information necessary to manage subscriptions, such as subscription status or transaction identifiers.

We do not intend to store full payment card numbers unless specifically required by an applicable payment service.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, loss, misuse, modification and destruction.

Security measures may include access controls, authentication, permissions, secure transmission and other technical and organisational safeguards.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Business Data Separation

CrewDeck is designed so workplace information is associated with the relevant business. Users should only be able to access information for businesses they are authorised to access, subject to their role and permissions.

13. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide CrewDeck, maintain business records, resolve disputes, maintain security, comply with legal obligations and operate the service.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted, anonymised or securely disposed of, subject to legal and operational requirements.

14. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their CrewDeck account and associated personal information through available account-deletion functionality or by contacting us.

Some workplace records may need to be retained by a business or by CrewDeck where required or permitted by law.

15. Access and Correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you and request correction of information that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.

16. Children's Privacy

CrewDeck is designed primarily for workplace and business use. We do not knowingly seek to collect unnecessary personal information from children.

If you believe personal information has been provided in circumstances where it should not have been collected, please contact us.

17. Overseas Storage and Processing

CrewDeck and its service providers may store or process information using infrastructure located outside Australia.

Where required by applicable privacy laws, we will take appropriate steps regarding overseas disclosure and processing.

18. Cookies and Similar Technologies

CrewDeck or its service providers may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers, analytics technologies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, improve security, remember preferences, operate features and improve performance.

19. Analytics and Technical Information

We may collect technical information such as device type, operating system, application version, error information, crash information, usage information and log information.

This information may be used to maintain security, diagnose problems and improve CrewDeck.

20. Privacy Choices

Depending on the features available, users may be able to access, correct or delete their information, update their profile, control certain notifications and contact us regarding privacy matters.

21. Privacy Complaints

If you believe we have handled your personal information incorrectly, please contact us using the contact details below.

In Australia, you may also have the right to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

22. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
