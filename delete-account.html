If you would like to delete your CrewDeck account and associated personal data, you can submit a deletion request using the instructions below.

How to request deletion

Send an account deletion request to the CrewDeck support email address. Include the email address associated with your CrewDeck account so we can identify the account.

Email: YOUR CREWDECK SUPPORT EMAIL

What will be deleted

When your account is deleted, we will delete or anonymise personal information associated with your account, including:

  • Your account and login information
  • Your profile information
  • Your profile picture, where applicable
  • Personal information associated with your CrewDeck account
  • Other personal data that can reasonably be associated with your account

Information that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, financial, or legitimate business purposes. Where information is retained, it will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose.

Processing your request

We may need to verify that the deletion request belongs to you before processing it. After verification, we will process the request within a reasonable period.

Important: Deleting your CrewDeck account may remove your access to businesses, rosters, shifts, messages, tasks, inventory information, and other features associated with your account.
